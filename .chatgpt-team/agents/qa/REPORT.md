### Independent technical assessment / precision
- `Decimal(value)` on **prevalidated ASCII digit text** is lossless and does not round at caller precision or use binary floats. `format(value,"f")` is applied only to finite Decimal with exponent exactly zero and validated canonical size; noncanonical internal representations fail rather than silently quantizing (e.g. `Decimal("1.0")` rejected even when mathematically integer).

Largest mathematical sum of **two** such values has at most **21 digits**; `localcontext` `prec=21` with `Rounded/Inexact` traps is therefore sufficient for exact intermediate addition. Result then passes independent **20-digit** boundary enforcement, otherwise `RialValueError`. The caller's original Decimal context is restored by `localcontext`. `sum_rials` checks each step and cannot silently carry an over-limit intermediate.
